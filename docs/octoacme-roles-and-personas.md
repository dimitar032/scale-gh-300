# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work with Product Managers to clarify requirements and acceptance criteria
- Coordinate with Project Managers on estimates, dependencies, and delivery risks
- Partner with UX/UI Designers or User Researchers to validate feasibility and implementation details
- Collaborate with Technical Leads or Architects on design and technical standards
- Build and maintain instrumentation with Data/Analytics Leads
- Address security and privacy requirements with Security/Privacy Leads
- Support QA/Testing with testability, defect fixes, and release verification

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Use UX/UI Designers or User Researchers to test customer needs and validate proposed solutions
- Align with Technical Leads or Architects on feasibility and technical trade-offs
- Work with Data/Analytics Leads to define outcome metrics and interpret evidence
- Coordinate with Security/Privacy Leads on compliance and customer-risk requirements
- Incorporate Customer Support/Operations feedback about customer impact and operational readiness
- Keep Stakeholders informed of priorities and trade-offs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with Delivery Leads or Scrum Masters on team cadence, impediment removal, and delivery visibility
- Work with Technical Leads or Architects to record technical risks, dependencies, and escalation needs
- Coordinate schedules and communication with the Product Manager and Stakeholders
- Include Security/Privacy Leads in risk decisions and escalations
- Coordinate release readiness with Customer Support/Operations Representatives

---

## QA/Testing

### Role Summary
QA/Testing validates that features meet quality standards and acceptance criteria. The role protects customer confidence through test planning, defect triage, and release verification.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain test strategies and acceptance checks
- Perform or automate functional, integration, and end-to-end testing
- Participate in backlog review and sprint planning
- Report defects, quality risks, and verification status
- Support release readiness and post-release verification

### Goals
- Prevent defects and detect regressions early
- Provide reliable evidence that acceptance criteria are met
- Improve test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Backlog and acceptance-criteria reviews
- Daily defect and risk updates
- Release verification reports

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work with Developers on testability and defect resolution
- Clarify expected behavior and success criteria with the Product Manager
- Coordinate test coverage and release risks with the Project Manager
- Validate implemented designs with UX/UI Designers or User Researchers
- Use Data/Analytics Leads to verify instrumentation and measurement
- Complete security and privacy verification with Security/Privacy Leads

---

## Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Stakeholders provide business context, requirements, approvals, and feedback. They help align project decisions with organizational priorities and affected user groups.

### Responsibilities
- Communicate needs, constraints, and decision criteria
- Review plans, outcomes, and major trade-offs
- Provide timely approvals and remove organizational blockers
- Participate in reviews and retrospectives as appropriate

### Goals
- Ensure the project aligns with organizational and customer needs
- Enable informed decisions at the right level
- Support successful adoption and outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Milestone updates and stakeholder reviews
- Decision logs and escalation channels
- Release announcements and feedback sessions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work primarily with Project Managers for plans, risks, decisions, and status
- Align with Product Managers on priorities and value outcomes
- Provide context to UX/UI Designers or User Researchers and Customer Support/Operations Representatives
- Support technical decisions, funding, or risk acceptance through Technical and Delivery Leads

---

## UX/UI Designer or User Researcher

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and User Researchers represent user needs throughout discovery and delivery. They create or validate interaction patterns, prototypes, and evidence about how users behave and what they value.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research or usability studies
- Define accessible, consistent interaction and interface designs
- Translate customer insights into journeys, prototypes, and design guidance
- Validate designs with users and maintain design decisions
- Collaborate on measurable usability and adoption outcomes

### Goals
- Improve usability, accessibility, and customer value
- Ground product decisions in evidence rather than assumptions
- Provide designs that can be implemented and tested consistently

### Typical Communication
- Research findings and design reviews
- Prototype walkthroughs and usability sessions
- Accessibility and interaction guidance

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to validate customer needs and shape priorities
- Provide feasible designs and clarifications to Developers
- Work with QA/Testing to define usability checks and acceptance criteria
- Share customer and usage findings with Stakeholders and Customer Support/Operations Representatives
- Coordinate instrumentation needs with Data/Analytics Leads

---

## Technical Lead or Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead or Architect guides system design, technical quality, and integration decisions while helping the team balance delivery needs with long-term maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical direction and architecture constraints
- Review major designs and integration approaches
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and trade-offs
- Promote engineering standards and code quality
- Support delivery planning and complex implementation decisions

### Goals
- Create reliable, maintainable, and scalable solutions
- Make technical trade-offs explicit and timely
- Reduce architectural and integration risk

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture records
- Risk, dependency, and trade-off discussions
- Code-review guidance and engineering standards

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work with Developers on implementation patterns and technical quality
- Explain trade-offs and risks to Product Managers and Project Managers
- Coordinate integration and operational requirements with Customer Support/Operations Representatives
- Partner with QA/Testing on test strategy and observability
- Advise Security/Privacy Leads on threat boundaries and control design

---

## Delivery Lead or Scrum Master

### Role Summary
The Delivery Lead or Scrum Master facilitates predictable team flow, planning, ceremonies, and impediment removal. This role strengthens delivery practices without replacing the Project Manager's cross-project or stakeholder responsibilities.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint or iteration planning and reviews
- Coach the team on delivery and continuous improvement practices
- Identify and remove delivery impediments
- Improve backlog readiness and team workflow
- Surface delivery trends and risks to project leadership

### Goals
- Improve flow, predictability, and team effectiveness
- Reduce avoidable work and cycle time
- Build a psychologically safe environment for feedback

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and planning sessions
- Delivery impediment and action-item reviews
- Sprint or milestone retrospectives

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Coordinate delivery status and escalations with Project Managers
- Work with Product Managers and Developers to keep priorities and capacity aligned
- Support QA/Testing and Technical Leads in defining ready work and resolving bottlenecks
- Facilitate follow-up on retrospective actions with the team and Stakeholders

---

## Data/Analytics Lead

### Role Summary
The Data/Analytics Lead makes product and delivery outcomes measurable. The role defines instrumentation, reporting, and analytical approaches for success metrics and operational signals.

### Responsibilities
- Translate success criteria into measurable events and metrics
- Design dashboards and analytical data models
- Validate data quality and measurement assumptions
- Interpret results for product and delivery decisions
- Identify missing instrumentation or measurement gaps

### Goals
- Produce trustworthy, actionable evidence
- Connect delivered work to customer and business outcomes
- Improve experiment and measurement quality

### Typical Communication
- Success-metrics and instrumentation reviews
- Dashboard and experiment reports
- Data-quality and analysis findings

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to define and monitor success metrics
- Work with Developers to instrument features and services
- Validate instrumentation and acceptance evidence with QA/Testing
- Share progress and risks with Project Managers and Stakeholders
- Collaborate with UX/UI Designers or User Researchers on behavioral evidence

---

## Security/Privacy Lead

### Role Summary
The Security/Privacy Lead identifies and mitigates security and privacy risks throughout discovery, implementation, release, and operation.

### Responsibilities
- Define applicable security and privacy requirements
- Conduct or coordinate threat and impact assessments
- Review designs, data flows, dependencies, and mitigations
- Guide testing, incident readiness, and remediation
- Support regulatory or policy compliance decisions

### Goals
- Reduce security and privacy risk
- Make control ownership and residual risk explicit
- Integrate protection into normal delivery work

### Typical Communication
- Security and privacy reviews
- Risk assessments and control guidance
- Incident and remediation updates

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Advise Technical Leads or Architects and Developers on secure design and implementation
- Work with QA/Testing on security and privacy verification
- Help Product Managers and Project Managers balance risk with product and delivery priorities
- Coordinate high-impact risks and incidents through established escalation paths
- Partner with Customer Support/Operations Representatives on operational and incident procedures

---

## Customer Support/Operations Representative

### Role Summary
The Customer Support/Operations Representative brings customer-impact and operational readiness perspectives into planning, delivery, and release activities.

### Responsibilities
- Represent support, operations, and customer feedback needs
- Define readiness materials, runbooks, and support workflows
- Identify likely customer issues and operational risks
- Participate in release verification and rollout planning
- Coordinate support responses and post-release monitoring

### Goals
- Improve supportability and operational readiness
- Reduce customer disruption during releases
- Accelerate issue detection and resolution

### Typical Communication
- Readiness and release reviews
- Support readiness documentation
- Customer-feedback and incident updates

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Share customer-impact insights with Product Managers and UX/UI Designers or User Researchers
- Coordinate release risks and schedules with Project Managers
- Help Developers and Technical Leads define runbooks, diagnostics, and supportability requirements
- Partner with QA/Testing on smoke tests and customer-impact scenarios
- Work with Security/Privacy Leads on incident and customer-communication procedures
- Keep Stakeholders informed of operational and support readiness

---

## Role Selection and Accountability

Projects should tailor the persona set to their scope and risk profile. One person may hold multiple roles on a smaller team, but the team should make the accountability explicit and avoid assigning conflicting work without a clear owner. Existing and new personas complement rather than replace each other: Product Managers define the outcome, Project Managers coordinate delivery and communication, Technical Leads and Delivery Leads guide technical and delivery practices, and the remaining personas provide specialized expertise and validation.
