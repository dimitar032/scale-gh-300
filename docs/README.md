# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Project Management Process Overview

OctoAcme follows a lightweight project lifecycle: initiation validates the business need, goals, stakeholders, success measures, and whether work is ready to proceed; planning turns approved initiatives into prioritized, estimated backlog items with acceptance criteria, a Definition of Done, dependencies, and milestones. During execution, teams track work on a shared project board, coordinate through standups and delivery syncs, and use small pull requests with issue links, review, and automated checks to deliver incrementally.

The core roles have clear, complementary responsibilities. Project Managers coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, and communications; Product Managers define outcomes, prioritize the backlog, and measure success; Developers build, test, review, and surface technical risks; QA/Testing validates acceptance criteria and quality; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Regular team and stakeholder updates, a shared source of project status, and escalation from the team through PM and Product Lead to sponsor help keep decisions, dependencies, and blockers visible.

Quality is integrated throughout delivery with unit and integration tests, end-to-end smoke tests for critical flows, CI linting and security scans, and manual QA where needed. Releases require acceptance criteria to be met, passing CI and security checks, release notes, a rollback or mitigation plan, and staging and production verification. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents capture learnings as owned, time-bound actions so teams can measure impact and continuously improve.
